Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

YouTube API Services

space-shorts-uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also bound by the Google Privacy Policy.

Scope

space-shorts-uploader is a single-user internal tool. Its only user is its author, who uses it to upload their own original videos to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What user information is accessed

With the author's permission, the tool obtains an OAuth token for the author's own Google account with the youtube.upload scope, and uses it only to call the videos.insert endpoint to upload the author's own videos. It does not read, list, collect, or analyze any YouTube data, and it accesses no other user's data.

How information is used, stored, and shared

The OAuth credentials are stored only on the author's own computer. They are never transmitted to, sold to, or shared with any third party. No other personal data is collected, processed, stored, or shared.

Advertising

The tool displays no advertising and no third party serves content or advertisements through it.

Cookies and device data

The tool does not use cookies and does not store, access, or collect any information on or from users' devices beyond the locally stored OAuth credentials described above.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access to your data at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Scope

space-shorts-uploader is a single-user internal tool used by its author to upload their own original videos to their own YouTube channel.

Data collected

None. The tool has no end users other than the author. It does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data, and does not retain any YouTube user data.

Credentials

OAuth credentials belong to the channel owner and are stored locally on the author's machine. They are never transmitted to third parties.

Revoking access

API access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
